# BlueBook Series Solutions

These are solutions to every single BlueBook problem on DMOJ. These problems, ranging from 3 points (e.g. Cross Country) and 7 points (e.g. Calculator) are worth 120
total points on DMOJ, not accounting for the DMOJ points scaling system.

The Bluebook series in general is rather easy, but due to the sheer quantity of questions it is worthwhile to include these.
