# CCC Solutions (From 1996 to Current - 2026)

Welcome to the most comprehensive repository of CCC solutions on the DMOJ / CEMC judges. From 1996 to 2025, this repo features 263 solutions which pass all their subtasks on both the CCC judge and DMOJ judge.

On DMOJ, these problems are worth a total of 1937 points (last update: September 26). Of course, this is not accounting for the scaling system (this would place you double the current top-placed user).

**Some Notes**

Some CCC solutions are from years which I actually participated - and those may seem to have less conformity to the 'Good Programming Practices', e.g. proper variable names,
spacing, etc. However, mostly you will find that all of my solutions are written in as shortened of a form as possible - it's just something I do which allows me to
type faster and get out submissions faster. So apologies, for any readability issues - I really didn't put much thought into how it looks, just the functionality.

Note that the time complexities written in the reports may be inaccurate - I didn't really pay too much attention to them. Most of them are absed on what I calculated to be the time complexity in-contest,
so some may be inaccurate. However, that being said, I attempted to give the time complexity to a certain sort of precision in oredr to differentiate the solution better.
For example, some solutions may be listed as `O(1000)`, which is essentially the same as `O(1)` (constant time), but the `O(1000)` puts it into perspective, I suppose.
